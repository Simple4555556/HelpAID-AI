HelpAid AI - Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 7, 2026

At HelpAid AI, we prioritize the privacy and security of your personal and medical information. This policy outlines how we collect, store, and process your data.

1. Data Collection
We collect the following information when you create a profile, save emergency contacts, check symptoms, or upload injury photos:
- Identity Information: Name, Age, Gender.
- Contact Details: Mobile Number, Email Address.
- Medical Information: Allergies, chronic diseases, previous injuries, active medications, and notes.
- Geolocation Coordinates: Used only in emergency SOS or location-based hospital searches with explicit user consent.
- Diagnostic Records: Symptoms check records, Gemini-analyzed injury scans, and AI Doctor chat transcripts.

2. Data Storage & Encryption
- All medical data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore and MongoDB collections, which utilize role-based access rules.
- Local in-memory fallbacks are used when database instances are unreachable, keeping logs confined to the client session.
- Diagnostic PDFs generated for doctors are compiled securely on the fly.

3. HIPAA & Medical Standards
- Although HelpAid AI is a first-response triage tool and not a clinical diagnosis system, we adopt HIPAA-compliant security patterns including encryption of data in transit and at rest.
- We do not share your profile with third-party networks without your express sharing commands (e.g., "Share with Doctor" or "Send SOS").

4. User Rights
- You can access, review, modify, or completely delete your HelpAid Profile at any time from your Secure Vault.
- If you have any inquiries regarding your medical data, contact our support team at privacy@helpaid.com.
